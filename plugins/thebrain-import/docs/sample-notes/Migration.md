# Move a knowledge map into notes

This sample export has eight thoughts about research, writing and learning.

## Check the source

- The two Plan thoughts have different IDs.
- One jump points to a missing thought.
- The research tag comes from a tag thought.
- The archive has two Markdown bodies and one text attachment.

## Check the result

Open Knowledge map to follow its child and jump links. Open Plan and Plan (2) to see two notes that kept their own links.
