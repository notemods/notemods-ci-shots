For vectors $\vect{x}, \vect{y} \in \R^n$ the inner product is

$$\inner{\vect{x}}{\vect{y}} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i y_i$$

and the norm is

$$\norm{\vect{x}} = \sqrt{\inner{\vect{x}}{\vect{x}}}$$

The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality:

$$\abs{\inner{\vect{x}}{\vect{y}}} \le \norm{\vect{x}} \, \norm{\vect{y}}$$

For a square matrix $A \in \R^{n \times n}$, $\tr A = \sum_{i} a_{ii}$ and $\tr(AB) = \tr(BA)$.
