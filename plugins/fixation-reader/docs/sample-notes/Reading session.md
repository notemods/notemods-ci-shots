# A quiet reading session

Long notes can hold a project plan, a book summary, or a careful account of a meeting. Fixation Reader adds emphasis to the start of words while the rest of the sentence stays readable.

## Make space for the main idea

The first paragraph explains the problem. The next paragraph gives the evidence. Read both before you choose an action. A short pause can help you connect the parts of the argument.

> A useful summary keeps the meaning of the source. It makes the next step clear.

## Keep the original text

The Markdown source stays unchanged. Links such as [[Reference desk]] keep their destination. Lists keep their structure.

- Read the question before the answer.
- Record the facts that support the decision.
- Return to the full note when the summary needs context.
