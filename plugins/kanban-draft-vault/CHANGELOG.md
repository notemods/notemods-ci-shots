## 1.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 7f23db6: Retention limits and the search field of draft recovery can now be selected without Pro. They show how to get Pro, with a Sponsor on GitHub button, and change nothing.

## 1.1.0

### Minor Changes

- After a minor or major update, Kanban Draft Vault opens one What's new tab in the background. The tab and settings card suggest other NoteMods plugins and Zovo Chat and link to notemods.com for feedback ([privacy](https://notemods.com/privacy/)). The tab sends no data. Turn the tab off in Settings. Add the Send feedback command. The plugin makes no new network requests.
