# Chapter 2: The logbook

## First entries

The logbook went back forty years. Most entries were short: wind, sea state, ships seen, hours of light. Some had a second line in pencil, added later, as if the writer had changed their mind.

## A letter in the drawer

Under the logbook she found an envelope. The stamp was old and the name on it was hers.

## Night watch

She climbed the tower at dusk and wound the clockwork. The lamp turned. Far out, a fishing boat answered with two short flashes.
