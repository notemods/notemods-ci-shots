# Chapter 1: Arrival

## The ferry

Mara came to the island on the last ferry of the season. The deck was wet and the wind pushed the rain sideways. She held her bag with both hands and watched the light on the point turn once, then again.

## The keeper's house

The house stood behind the tower, low and white, with a green door that did not close well. Inside it smelled of salt and lamp oil. On the table was a note in a careful hand: the keys, the logbook, and a list of things that must never be forgotten.
