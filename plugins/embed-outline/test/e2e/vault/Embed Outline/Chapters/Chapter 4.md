# Chapter 4: Winter

## Short days

By December the light came late and left early. She kept the lamp burning sixteen hours a day.

## The storm

The storm came in on the night of the new moon. The barometer fell faster than she had ever seen.

### The lamp fails

At two in the morning the clockwork stopped. She turned the lens by hand for four hours.

### Morning

When the sky cleared, a trawler lay safe in the harbour. Its skipper climbed the hill to shake her hand.

## After

The village never spoke of the storm again, but the bread was always free.
