# a WEEK of field work in the north

## getting there by train and ferry

Two trains and one ferry, nine hours in total. The ferry runs twice a day in October.

## The First Survey Site

Wet ground, three plots, eighteen samples. We marked every plot with a numbered stake.

## what went wrong with the gps unit

The battery cover cracked on day two. Tape held it for the rest of the week.

## notes for next year's team

Bring spare batteries and a second dry bag.
